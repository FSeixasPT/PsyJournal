First PsyJournal release, a fork of PsychonautWiki Journal 11.11 with a new icon.
• Live timeline notification and home-screen widgets
• Preview your next dose on the timeline
• Custom colors; hide substances or ingestions from timelines
• Change an ingestion's substance and route
• Duration and counts in journal rows
• Quick times like "15 min ago" when logging
• Hide unused routes, merge routes into one line
• More complete interaction warnings
• Targets Android 16, reproducible builds
