# PsyJournal privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

PsyJournal is a substance journal for harm reduction. It is built so that what you record stays with you.

## What the app collects

Nothing. PsyJournal has no account, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting, and it doesn't send any data to the developer or to anyone else. The app doesn't even have permission to use the internet.

## What stays on your device

Everything you enter, such as ingestions, doses, notes, ratings, locations you type in and custom substances, is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

The substance information (doses, durations, interactions and safer-use tips) is built into the app. Looking it up doesn't contact any server.

## When data leaves your device

Only when you choose to:

- **Export:** Settings → Export File saves your journal as a file in the place you pick. Where it goes from there is up to you.
- **Links:** links in the app, such as PsychonautWiki articles or drug-checking services, open in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.
- **Device backup:** if you have turned on your phone's own backup (for example Google backup), Android may include PsyJournal's data in it. That backup is handled by your phone's system and its provider under your settings, not by PsyJournal.

## Permissions

PsyJournal asks for one permission: **notifications**, used only for the optional live timeline notification. You can turn it off at any time.

## Children

PsyJournal is meant for adults. It doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. The history of this file is public in the project's repository.
